I am a PhD candidate at the group of [Logic of Autonomous Dynamical Systems](https://logic.kastel.kit.edu/team/) at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT). Head of group is [Prof. Dr. André Platzer](https://symbolaris.com/).

I received my Master's and Bachelor's degree in Mathematics from the University of Stuttgart. My Master thesis was supervised by [Associate Professor Vera Koponen](https://www.uu.se/en/contact-and-organisation/staff?query=N96-558) from Uppsala University, Sweden during an ERASMUS stay abroad.

Furthermore, I hold two Bachelor's degrees from the University of Music Karlsruhe, one in music theory and one in school music. I teach music theory at the University of Music Karlsruhe.
